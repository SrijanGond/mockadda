Eligibility

  • Primary – Urban: graduation plus a recognised teacher training qualification (BTC, D.El.Ed, B.Ed, special B.T.C. or B.El.Ed as listed in the advertisement) and a pass in a TET run by the state or central government.
  • Associated Primary (aided): graduation, BTC or D.El.Ed, and a pass in UP TET (classes 1 to 5).
  • You must fill your school-type preference in the application. It cannot be changed after the last date.
  • Both the registration and application form must be filled in English.

Age limit (as on 1 July 2026)

  • Minimum age: 21 years for both posts.
  • Urban post: maximum 40 years. Relaxation: 5 years for UP SC, ST and OBC; 15 years for UP persons with disability; 3 years extra over service for ex-servicemen; up to 60 years for Shiksha Mitras.
  • Aided post: retirement age is 62 years.

How to apply

  • Do One Time Registration (OTR) on upessc.up.gov.in, then fill the application.
  • Upload a photo (10–200 KB), signature (JPG/JPEG) and PDF documents of 50–500 KB.
  • Pay the fee by card or net banking and keep the receipt. An application without paid fee is not valid.
  • Take a print of the submitted form. You need it with your documents at verification.
